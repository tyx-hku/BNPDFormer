Heterogeneous factory graph                       Existing studies are limited by the scarcity of bottleneck events in physical factories and by data that
Digital twin simulation                           predominantly describe machines while underrepresenting workers, buffers, materials, and logistics.
                                                  Consequently, conventional time-series models and local graph predictors may not fully capture
                                                  heterogeneous cross-resource interaction or the delayed propagation of a disturbance into a remote
                                                  station-level constraint. This paper proposes PHAST-DP, a process-informed heterogeneous-graph
                                                  spatiotemporal Transformer for fine-grained dynamic bottleneck prediction with delayed propagation.
                                                  A disturbance-controlled digital production line generates traceable human-, material-, machine-,
                                                  and logistics-related trajectories without disrupting physical production. Process-informed labelling
                                                  and clustering distinguish sustained bottleneck regimes from normal production patterns, improving
                                                  data separability and providing operational evidence for cause prediction. A heterogeneous factory
                                                  graph preserves typed process and logistics relations, while recurrent short-term patterns condition
                                                  spatiotemporal interaction so that lagged precursors can contribute to prediction. PHAST-DP jointly
                                                  reports the bottleneck station, onset, duration, process cause, and remaining order time at minute
                                                  resolution. On the held-out simulation set, report F1 is 0.804∕0.778∕0.768 for 5/10/15-min onset caps,
                                                  with upcoming-event recall near 0.53. Removing clustering from the data pipeline lowers report F1
                                                  to 0.735∕0.646∕0.637 and upcoming recall to 0.299∕0.324∕0.320, supporting improved operational
                                                  separability and prediction accuracy from the combined labelling–clustering strategy. Directional
                                                  entity and delay-pattern ablations further associate heterogeneous observations, cross-type relations,
                                                  and recurrent pattern conditioning with stronger early warning and process diagnosis. These results
                                                  support a fine-grained and operationally traceable approach to dynamic bottleneck prediction.
                                                  动态瓶颈预测对于数字制造具有重要意义，因为及时获知不断变化的吞吐约束，能
                                                  够使生产干预在其影响扩散至整条产线之前实施。该任务不仅需要判断未来由哪
                                                  一资源限制吞吐量，还需要说明事件何时开始、持续多久，以及何种可观测生产
                                                  状态能够解释该事件。现有研究受到两方面限制：实体工厂中的瓶颈事件样本稀
                                                  缺；可获取数据又主要描述机器，对人员、缓冲区、物料和物流的表征不足。因此，
                                                  传统时间序列模型和局部图预测方法难以充分捕捉异构资源之间的交互，以及局
                                                  部扰动经过一定时延后演变为远端工位约束的过程。为此，本文提出 PHAST-DP，
                                                  一种面向延迟传播动态瓶颈预测的过程启发式异构图时空 Transformer。可控扰动
                                                  的数字化生产线在不干扰实体生产的条件下生成可追溯的人员、物料、机器和物
                                                  流轨迹。过程启发式标签与聚类相结合，用于区分持续性瓶颈工况与正常生产模
                                                  式，从而提高数据可分性，并为原因预测提供运营证据。异构工厂图保留具有类型
                                                  差异的工艺和物流关系，重复出现的短时模式进一步调节时空交互，使具有时滞
                                                  的前驱信息能够参与预测。PHAST-DP 以分钟为分辨率，联合报告瓶颈工位、开始
                                                  时间、持续时间、过程原因和订单剩余时间。在留出仿真测试集上，五/十/十五分
                                                  钟起点范围的 report F1 分别为 0.804∕0.778∕0.768，upcoming 事件召回率约为 0.53。
                                                  从数据流程中移除聚类后，report F1 降至 0.735∕0.646∕0.637，upcoming 召回率降
                                                  至 0.299∕0.324∕0.320，支持过程标签—聚类组合能够提高运营状态可分性和预测
                                                  准确率。方向性多实体与延迟模式消融进一步表明，异构观测、跨类型关系和重复
                                                  模式条件化与更好的提前预警及过程诊断相关。上述结果支持一种细粒度且具有
                                                  运营可追溯性的动态瓶颈预测方法。
