\begin{abstract}
Dynamic bottleneck prediction is important to digital manufacturing because timely knowledge of a changing throughput constraint enables production intervention before its effects spread across the line. The task is to determine which resource will constrain throughput and to characterize when the event begins, how long it persists, and what observable production condition explains it. Existing studies are limited by the scarcity of bottleneck events in physical factories and by data that predominantly describe machines while underrepresenting workers, buffers, materials, and logistics. Consequently, conventional time-series models and local graph predictors may not fully capture heterogeneous cross-resource interaction or the delayed propagation of a disturbance into a remote station-level constraint. This paper proposes \textbf{BNPDFormer}, a delay-aware spatiotemporal Transformer for fine-grained dynamic bottleneck prediction. A disturbance-controlled digital production line generates traceable human-, material-, machine-, and logistics-related trajectories without disrupting physical production. Process-informed labelling and clustering distinguish sustained bottleneck regimes from normal production patterns, improving data separability and providing operational evidence for cause prediction. A heterogeneous factory graph preserves typed process and logistics relations, while recurrent short-term patterns condition spatiotemporal interaction so that lagged precursors can contribute to prediction. BNPDFormer jointly reports the bottleneck station, onset, duration, process cause, and remaining order time at minute resolution. On the held-out simulation set, report F1 is $0.804/0.778/0.768$ for 5/10/15-min onset caps, with upcoming-event recall near $0.53$. Removing clustering from the data pipeline lowers report F1 to $0.735/0.646/0.637$ and upcoming recall to $0.299/0.324/0.320$, supporting improved operational separability and prediction accuracy from the combined labelling--clustering strategy. Directional entity and delay-pattern ablations further associate heterogeneous observations, cross-type relations, and recurrent pattern conditioning with stronger early warning and process diagnosis. These results support a fine-grained and operationally traceable approach to dynamic bottleneck prediction.

\zhpara{动态瓶颈预测对于数字制造具有重要意义，因为及时获知不断变化的吞吐约束，能够使生产干预在其影响扩散至整条产线之前实施。该任务不仅需要判断未来由哪一资源限制吞吐量，还需要说明事件何时开始、持续多久，以及何种可观测生产状态能够解释该事件。现有研究受到两方面限制：实体工厂中的瓶颈事件样本稀缺；可获取数据又主要描述机器，对人员、缓冲区、物料和物流的表征不足。因此，传统时间序列模型和局部图预测方法难以充分捕捉异构资源之间的交互，以及局部扰动经过一定时延后演变为远端工位约束的过程。为此，本文提出 \textbf{BNPDFormer}，一种面向细粒度动态瓶颈预测的延迟感知时空 Transformer。可控扰动的数字化生产线在不干扰实体生产的条件下生成可追溯的人员、物料、机器和物流轨迹。过程启发式标签与聚类相结合，用于区分持续性瓶颈工况与正常生产模式，从而提高数据可分性，并为原因预测提供运营证据。异构工厂图保留具有类型差异的工艺和物流关系，重复出现的短时模式进一步调节时空交互，使具有时滞的前驱信息能够参与预测。BNPDFormer 以分钟为分辨率，联合报告瓶颈工位、开始时间、持续时间、过程原因和订单剩余时间。在留出仿真测试集上，五/十/十五分钟起点范围的 report F1 分别为 $0.804/0.778/0.768$，upcoming 事件召回率约为 $0.53$。从数据流程中移除聚类后，report F1 降至 $0.735/0.646/0.637$，upcoming 召回率降至 $0.299/0.324/0.320$，支持过程标签—聚类组合能够提高运营状态可分性和预测准确率。方向性多实体与延迟模式消融进一步表明，异构观测、跨类型关系和重复模式条件化与更好的提前预警及过程诊断相关。上述结果支持一种细粒度且具有运营可追溯性的动态瓶颈预测方法。}

\section{Introduction} \label{sec:intro}
%% ========================================================================

Digital manufacturing systems coordinate machines, workers, materials, buffers, and autonomous handling resources through tightly coupled process and logistics flows. This coupling improves production flexibility, but it also allows a local disturbance to accumulate as work-in-process, interrupt material supply, delay handling services, and eventually constrain throughput at another station. Retrospective bottleneck detection may therefore leave insufficient time for material replenishment, labour reallocation, equipment maintenance, buffer adjustment, or logistics rerouting. Predicting how a constraint forms and propagates across heterogeneous factory resources is essential for converting an emerging disruption into an actionable production warning.

\zhpara{数字制造系统通过紧密耦合的工艺流与物流流，协调机器、人员、物料、缓冲区和自主搬运资源。这种耦合提高了生产柔性，但也使局部扰动可能逐步造成在制品积压、物料供应中断和搬运服务延误，并最终在另一工位形成吞吐约束。因此，事后瓶颈检测往往无法为物料补充、人员调配、设备维护、缓冲区调整或物流重路由留出足够时间。预测约束如何在异构工厂资源之间形成和传播，是将正在发展的生产扰动转化为可执行预警的关键。}

A production bottleneck is the resource or process that limits system throughput at a given time~\cite{roser2001practical,subramaniyan2023throughput,tang2024comprehensive}. Its identity and location are not fixed: changes in resource availability, material supply, work-in-process, and operating conditions can cause the bottleneck to migrate during production~\cite{roser2002shifting,lai2021data}. We investigate station-level dynamic bottleneck prediction on a digital-twin shared production line for water-pipe manufacturing. Historical and current observations are used to predict the affected station, event onset and duration, observable process cause, and remaining order time. The objective is not only to identify a future bottleneck, but also to provide a fine-grained and interpretable report that supports timely intervention.

\zhpara{生产瓶颈是指在某一时刻限制系统吞吐量的资源或过程~\cite{roser2001practical,subramaniyan2023throughput,tang2024comprehensive}。其身份与位置并非固定不变；资源可用性、物料供给、在制品和运行条件的变化，都可能使瓶颈在生产过程中发生迁移~\cite{roser2002shifting,lai2021data}。本文在一条用于水管制造的数字孪生共享产线上研究工位级动态瓶颈预测，利用历史和当前观测预测受影响工位、事件开始时间与持续时间、可观测过程原因以及订单剩余时间。研究目标不仅是识别未来瓶颈，还要形成支持及时干预的细粒度、可解释报告。}

Despite progress in bottleneck prediction, three limitations remain. First, existing studies predominantly model machine or workstation data. Time-series and recurrent methods forecast active periods, blockage, starvation, or bottleneck scores from machine-centred observations~\cite{li2011throughput,subramaniyan2018data,lai2018twolayer,liu2023lstmdam}; graph-based methods further introduce workstation, task, or material-flow relations~\cite{lai2023bstan,yin2025btgcn,ma2023tusb,cen2025dstnn,wu2025knowledge}. Nevertheless, workers, material availability, buffers, gantries, and transport robots are rarely represented together, even though their interactions can determine where and when a throughput constraint appears. Second, bottleneck supervision is difficult to construct. Positive events are scarce in normal operation, and deliberately inducing failures, worker absence, material shortages, or logistics interruption in a physical factory is costly and disruptive. Conventional scalar scores and turning-point indicators derived from utilization, blockage, or starvation~\cite{li2009data,subramaniyan2023throughput,lai2021data,su2022dynamic} can support retrospective analysis, but isolated threshold crossings may confuse transient variation with a sustained constraint and provide limited evidence about the operational cause. This weak separation between normal and bottleneck states complicates learning and reduces the interpretability of model outputs. Third, dynamic bottlenecks exhibit delayed cross-resource propagation. A disturbance may pass through transport and buffering before emerging as blockage or starvation at a downstream station, so the resource where the disturbance originates may differ from the resource that later constrains throughput. Recurrent transmission and local graph aggregation can capture temporal or neighbourhood dependence, but do not explicitly align an earlier cross-resource pattern with its delayed consequence. These limitations motivate a unified approach to heterogeneous observation, process-consistent supervision, and delay-aware prediction.

\zhpara{尽管瓶颈预测已取得进展，现有研究仍存在三方面不足。第一，已有研究主要建模机器或工位数据。时间序列和循环方法通常依据以机器为中心的观测预测活跃期、阻塞、饥饿或瓶颈评分~\cite{li2011throughput,subramaniyan2018data,lai2018twolayer,liu2023lstmdam}；图模型进一步引入工位、任务或物料流关系~\cite{lai2023bstan,yin2025btgcn,ma2023tusb,cen2025dstnn,wu2025knowledge}。然而，人员、物料可用性、缓冲区、龙门和运输机器人很少被纳入统一表示，尽管这些资源之间的交互会共同决定吞吐约束在何处、何时出现。第二，瓶颈监督数据难以构造。正常生产中的瓶颈正样本稀少，而在实体工厂主动制造设备故障、人员离岗、物料短缺或物流中断成本高且会干扰生产。由利用率、阻塞或饥饿构成的传统标量评分与转折点指标~\cite{li2009data,subramaniyan2023throughput,lai2021data,su2022dynamic}能够支持事后分析，但孤立阈值穿越容易混淆瞬时波动与持续性约束，并且对运营原因提供的证据有限。正常状态与瓶颈状态之间可分性不足，不仅增加模型学习难度，也削弱预测结果的可解释性。第三，动态瓶颈具有跨资源延迟传播属性。扰动可能经过运输和缓冲后，才在下游工位表现为阻塞或饥饿，因此扰动起源资源可能不同于随后限制吞吐量的资源。循环传递和局部图聚合能够刻画时间或邻域依赖，却没有显式对齐更早的跨资源模式与其延迟后果。上述不足共同要求一种能够统一处理异构观测、过程一致监督和延迟感知预测的方法。}

To address these limitations, we propose \textbf{BNPDFormer}. A disturbance-controlled digital-twin production line provides traceable trajectories of machines, workers, material supply, buffers, gantries, and transport robots without interrupting physical production~\cite{lu2020digital,isaacsim2026,xue2026safe}. These observations are organized as a heterogeneous process--logistics graph: machines and handling resources are represented as interacting entities, while material shortage, buffer occupancy, labour availability, and transport delay provide resource-specific operational context. Process-informed rules then combine queue, blockage, starvation, availability, shortage, and logistics evidence with persistence requirements to construct sustained bottleneck events. Clustering complements these rules by grouping recurrent operating regimes and short-term precursor patterns, thereby increasing the separability of bottleneck and normal states and linking predictions to recognizable production evidence rather than an opaque score. Finally, a delay-pattern module is incorporated into a spatiotemporal Transformer adapted from PDFormer~\cite{jiang2023pdformer}. The module uses recurrent short-term patterns to condition cross-resource attention, enabling earlier resource states to contribute to the prediction of constraints that emerge elsewhere after a lag. The resulting model produces a minute-level report of bottleneck station, onset, duration, observable process cause, and remaining order time.

\zhpara{为解决上述问题，本文提出 \textbf{BNPDFormer}。可控扰动的数字孪生生产线在不干扰实体生产的条件下，提供机器、人员、物料供给、缓冲区、龙门和运输机器人的可追溯轨迹~\cite{lu2020digital,isaacsim2026,xue2026safe}。这些观测被组织为异构工艺—物流图：机器与搬运资源作为相互作用的实体，物料短缺、缓冲区占用、人员可用性和运输延迟则提供与资源类型相关的运营上下文。随后，过程启发式规则将队列、阻塞、饥饿、可用性、缺料和物流证据与持续性条件结合，构造持续性瓶颈事件。聚类作为规则的补充，对重复出现的运行工况和短时前驱模式进行分组，从而提高瓶颈状态与正常状态的可分性，并使预测能够对应可识别的生产证据，而不是停留在不透明的评分层面。最后，本文在由 PDFormer~\cite{jiang2023pdformer}改造的时空 Transformer 中加入延迟模式模块。该模块利用重复短时模式调节跨资源注意力，使较早资源状态能够参与预测经过一定时滞后在其他位置显现的约束。最终模型生成分钟级瓶颈报告，包括瓶颈工位、开始时间、持续时间、可观测过程原因和订单剩余时间。}

The contributions of this work are summarized as follows. (1) We extend dynamic bottleneck prediction from machine-centred observations to heterogeneous multi-entity factory data. Machines, workers, material-supply states, buffers, gantries, and transport robots are jointly represented with their process and logistics relations, enabling the model to capture bottlenecks arising from production, labour, material, and handling interactions rather than from machine states alone. (2) We develop a process-informed labelling and clustering strategy for bottleneck data whose positive events are scarce and weakly separable from normal fluctuations. Resource-specific rules and persistence constraints provide operationally meaningful event labels, while clustering organizes recurrent regimes and precursor patterns. Their combination improves data separability and supports interpretation through observable evidence associated with event formation and process cause. (3) We propose a delay-aware spatiotemporal Transformer for dynamic bottleneck propagation. Its delay-pattern module conditions cross-resource interaction on relevant historical patterns, allowing the model to associate an upstream precursor with a downstream constraint that appears after transport, buffering, or service delay.

\zhpara{本文的主要贡献如下。（1）将动态瓶颈预测从以机器为中心的观测扩展到工厂异构多实体数据。机器、人员、物料供给状态、缓冲区、龙门和运输机器人及其工艺与物流关系被纳入联合表示，使模型能够捕捉由生产、人员、物料和搬运交互形成的瓶颈，而不再只依赖机器状态。（2）针对瓶颈正事件稀缺、且与正常生产波动可分性不足的问题，提出过程启发式标签与聚类相结合的数据构造策略。资源类型相关规则与持续性约束提供具有运营含义的事件标签，聚类则组织重复出现的运行工况和前驱模式。两者结合提高数据可分性，并通过与事件形成和过程原因相关的可观测证据支持预测解释。（3）针对动态瓶颈的延迟传播问题，提出延迟感知时空 Transformer。其延迟模式模块依据相关历史模式调节跨资源交互，使模型能够将上游前驱状态与经过运输、缓冲或服务时延后出现的下游约束联系起来。}

\section{Literature review} \label{sec:review}
%% ========================================================================

\subsection{Dynamic bottlenecks and the prediction task}

A throughput bottleneck is the resource or process that constrains system output, but its identification depends on both the evidence used and the time scale of observation. Existing detection methods draw on queue states, process states, or combinations of the two, and no single indicator is universally valid across manufacturing configurations~\cite{subramaniyan2023throughput}. High utilization, a long queue, or a machine failure is therefore evidence of a possible constraint rather than sufficient proof that the resource limits line throughput. Roser et al.\ formalized active-period-based detection and subsequently showed that the constraining resource can shift over time~\cite{roser2001practical,roser2002shifting}. This distinction is important because long-run averages can conceal short-lived but operationally consequential bottleneck migration.

\zhpara{吞吐瓶颈是限制系统产出的资源或过程，但其识别结果同时取决于所用证据和观察时间尺度。现有检测方法利用队列状态、过程状态或二者的组合，且不存在适用于所有制造系统配置的单一指标~\cite{subramaniyan2023throughput}。因此，高利用率、长队列或设备故障只能表明资源可能形成约束，不能单独证明其限制了整线吞吐量。Roser 等首先形式化了基于活跃期的检测方法，随后说明制约资源会随时间迁移~\cite{roser2001practical,roser2002shifting}。这一区分十分重要，因为长期平均值可能掩盖持续时间较短但具有运营影响的瓶颈迁移。}

Detection and prediction consequently address different questions. Detection locates a constraint that has already appeared, whereas prediction uses historical and current observations to estimate a future constrained state. Subramaniyan et al.\ distinguish identification, diagnosis, prediction, and prescription as separate stages of AI-supported bottleneck analysis~\cite{subramaniyan2021ai}. Early prediction first extrapolated bottleneck indicators: time-series and active-period methods estimated which machine would become throughput-critical~\cite{li2011throughput,subramaniyan2018data}, and proactive dispatching used future bottleneck estimates to support task assignment~\cite{huang2019proactive}. More recent work incorporates production relations and changing operating conditions. Liu et al.\ combine a supply--demand-derived indicator with a dual-attention LSTM, concept-drift detection, and transfer learning~\cite{liu2023lstmdam}; Ma et al.\ jointly model spatial and temporal information in high-variety shops with complex routings~\cite{ma2023tusb}; and Lai et al.\ forecast blockage and starvation before inferring the future bottleneck~\cite{lai2023bstan}. The field has thus progressed from univariate indicator extrapolation toward relational prediction under evolving shop-floor conditions.

\zhpara{因此，瓶颈检测与瓶颈预测回答不同问题。检测定位已经出现的约束，预测则利用历史和当前观测估计未来受限状态。Subramaniyan 等将人工智能支持的瓶颈分析区分为识别、诊断、预测和处方四个阶段~\cite{subramaniyan2021ai}。早期预测主要外推瓶颈指标：时间序列和活跃期方法估计将成为吞吐约束的机器~\cite{li2011throughput,subramaniyan2018data}，主动任务分派则利用未来瓶颈估计辅助任务配置~\cite{huang2019proactive}。近期研究进一步纳入生产关系和运行条件变化。Liu 等将供需关系构造的指标与双注意力 LSTM、概念漂移检测和迁移学习结合~\cite{liu2023lstmdam}；Ma 等在复杂工艺路线的多品种车间联合建模空间与时间信息~\cite{ma2023tusb}；Lai 等先预测阻塞和饥饿，再推断未来瓶颈~\cite{lai2023bstan}。由此，相关研究已从单变量指标外推逐步发展为面向变化现场条件的关系预测。}

For production intervention, however, a predicted score or resource identity alone is incomplete. A warning should specify where the constraint will occur, whether it is already continuing or is yet to begin, when it will start, and how long it is expected to persist. It should also distinguish an observable process cause from the external disturbance that may have initiated the event. This event-oriented view motivates the station, onset, duration, cause, and remaining-order-time targets formalized in Section~\ref{sec:problem}.

\zhpara{然而，对于生产干预，仅预测一个评分或资源身份仍不完整。预警还应说明约束将在何处出现、属于正在持续还是尚未开始、何时开始以及预计持续多久，并区分可观测的过程原因与可能触发事件的外部扰动。这种事件导向的观点引出了第~\ref{sec:problem}节所定义的工位、起点、持续时间、原因和订单剩余时间目标。}

\subsection{Bottleneck indicators and input representation}

Constructing a predictive state from production records requires explicit choices of resource scope, temporal aggregation, and indicator semantics. Queue length and waiting time describe accumulation; activity and utilization describe resource engagement; and blockage, starvation, and downtime describe different mechanisms of lost productive time~\cite{subramaniyan2023throughput,lai2021data}. Active-period methods additionally preserve temporal continuity. The average active period was introduced as a practical bottleneck criterion~\cite{roser2001practical} and later adapted to real-time production data~\cite{subramaniyan2016realtime}. Compared with a point state or a window average, a continuous run records persistence and is therefore better aligned with the difference between a transient fluctuation and a sustained constraint.

\zhpara{从生产记录构造预测状态，需要明确资源范围、时间聚合方式和指标语义。队列长度与等待时间描述积压，活动率与利用率描述资源投入程度，阻塞、饥饿和停机则对应不同的有效生产时间损失机制~\cite{subramaniyan2023throughput,lai2021data}。活跃期方法进一步保留时间连续性：平均活跃期最初被提出作为实用瓶颈判据~\cite{roser2001practical}，之后又被扩展到实时生产数据~\cite{subramaniyan2016realtime}。与时刻状态或窗口平均值相比，连续热段能够记录持续性，因而更适合区分瞬时波动与持续约束。}

Multivariate indicators can be organized by process knowledge or by empirical similarity. Process-informed methods use supply--demand relations, blockage, starvation, and factory structure to give a state operational meaning~\cite{lai2021data,liu2023lstmdam}. Data-driven grouping instead searches for recurrent regimes. Subramaniyan et al.\ generate machine-level bottleneck-indicator series and group machines with similar dynamics using dynamic time warping and hierarchical clustering~\cite{subramaniyan2020clustering}. K-means offers a simpler partition for fixed-dimensional window samples, although its result depends on feature scaling, distance, and the selected number of clusters~\cite{jain2010clustering}. These approaches answer different questions: trajectory clustering compares resources over time, while window clustering compares operating conditions at particular periods. In either case, a cluster is not inherently a throughput-bottleneck class and requires validation against production evidence.

\zhpara{多维指标可以依据过程知识或数据相似性进行组织。过程启发式方法利用供需关系、阻塞、饥饿和工厂结构赋予状态明确的运营含义~\cite{lai2021data,liu2023lstmdam}；数据驱动分组则寻找重复出现的工况。Subramaniyan 等从事件日志生成机器级瓶颈指标序列，并使用动态时间规整与层次聚类对具有相似动态的机器分组~\cite{subramaniyan2020clustering}。对于固定维度的窗口样本，K-means 提供了更简单的划分方法，但结果取决于特征尺度、距离度量和簇数选择~\cite{jain2010clustering}。两类方法回答的问题不同：轨迹聚类比较资源随时间的变化，窗口聚类比较特定时段的运行工况。无论采用何种方法，簇本身都不天然等同于吞吐瓶颈类别，仍需通过生产证据加以验证。}

Predictors must finally combine continuous measurements, categorical resource identity, and derived operating states. The Temporal Fusion Transformer demonstrates one general strategy: categorical variables are embedded, continuous variables are projected, and variable-selection networks combine them in a common latent space~\cite{lim2021tft}. For manufacturing, this suggests that raw intensities and derived regimes should be complementary rather than interchangeable. It also motivates preserving type semantics when machines, workers, buffers, and handling agents are encoded together, as done by the grouped representation in Section~\ref{sec:method}.

\zhpara{预测模型最终需要联合处理连续测量、资源类别和派生运行状态。Temporal Fusion Transformer 提供了一种通用方案：对类别变量进行嵌入，对连续变量进行投影，再由变量选择网络在共同潜空间中组合~\cite{lim2021tft}。对于制造场景，这表明原始强度与派生工况应当互为补充，而不是相互替代；当机器、人员、缓冲区和搬运主体被共同编码时，还应保留其类型语义。这也构成第~\ref{sec:method}节分组表示设计的依据。}

\subsection{Graph neural networks for spatiotemporal manufacturing data}

Process precedence, material transfer, shared labour, and handling services naturally form a resource network. Graph neural networks learn node representations by propagating and aggregating information over such relations~\cite{wu2021gnn}. Graph convolutional networks use normalized graph structure for neighbourhood aggregation~\cite{kipf2017gcn}, whereas graph attention networks learn feature-dependent neighbour weights~\cite{velickovic2018gat}. Neither mechanism alone represents temporal evolution, so spatiotemporal forecasting architectures combine graph aggregation with recurrent or convolutional sequence models.

\zhpara{工艺先后、物料转移、共享劳动力和搬运服务自然形成资源网络。图神经网络通过在这些关系上传播和聚合信息学习节点表示~\cite{wu2021gnn}。图卷积网络利用归一化图结构进行邻域聚合~\cite{kipf2017gcn}，图注意力网络则学习由节点特征决定的邻居权重~\cite{velickovic2018gat}。两种机制本身都不能完整表示时间演化，因此时空预测架构通常将图聚合与循环或卷积序列模型结合。}

Traffic forecasting provides well-established examples of this design space. DCRNN embeds diffusion convolution in an encoder--decoder recurrent network to model directed spatial propagation and temporal dynamics~\cite{li2018dcrnn}. STGCN replaces recurrence with graph convolution and gated temporal convolution~\cite{yu2018stgcn}. Graph WaveNet learns an adaptive adjacency from node embeddings and uses dilated causal temporal convolution~\cite{wu2019graphwavenet}, while STSGCN constructs local graphs across adjacent time steps so that spatial and temporal neighbours are aggregated jointly~\cite{song2020stsgcn}. These models show that topology, learned similarity, and temporal organization are distinct modelling choices rather than a single ``spatiotemporal'' operation.

\zhpara{交通预测为这一设计空间提供了成熟范例。DCRNN 将扩散卷积嵌入编码器—解码器循环网络，以联合建模有向空间传播和时间动态~\cite{li2018dcrnn}；STGCN 使用图卷积与门控时间卷积替代循环结构~\cite{yu2018stgcn}；Graph WaveNet 从节点嵌入学习自适应邻接，并采用膨胀因果时间卷积~\cite{wu2019graphwavenet}；STSGCN 则在相邻时刻之间构造局部图，联合聚合空间和时间邻居~\cite{song2020stsgcn}。这些模型说明，拓扑、学习得到的相似性和时间组织是彼此不同的建模选择，而不是单一的“时空”操作。}

Manufacturing studies instantiate these choices with different production objects and targets. BSTAN represents workstations and line relations, then combines graph attention with a GRU to forecast blockage and starvation before bottleneck identification~\cite{lai2023bstan}. BTGCN constructs a task graph from shared operators and combines graph convolution with a GRU to forecast task execution frequency~\cite{yin2025btgcn}. Dual-BDSTN uses recurrent and self-attentive temporal representations, a dynamic material-flow graph, and cross-attention to predict blockage and starvation time~\cite{cen2025dstnn}. Other recent models integrate routing information or production knowledge into spatial--temporal prediction~\cite{ma2023tusb,wu2025knowledge}. Their nodes, edges, and outputs have different operational meanings, so architectural comparisons are valid only when these task definitions and evaluation protocols are aligned.

\zhpara{制造研究则以不同生产对象和目标实现上述思路。BSTAN 表示工位及产线关系，并结合图注意力与 GRU 预测阻塞和饥饿，再进行瓶颈判断~\cite{lai2023bstan}；BTGCN 依据共享操作员构造任务图，并结合图卷积与 GRU 预测任务执行频次~\cite{yin2025btgcn}；Dual-BDSTN 使用循环与自注意时间表示、动态物料流图和交叉注意力预测阻塞与饥饿时间~\cite{cen2025dstnn}。近期其他模型还将工艺路线或生产知识纳入时空预测~\cite{ma2023tusb,wu2025knowledge}。由于这些研究中的节点、边和输出具有不同运营含义，只有在任务定义和评价协议对齐时，架构比较才具有有效性。}

A remaining limitation is that the receptive field and temporal evidence are determined by the graph construction and the coupling between spatial and temporal modules. Local message passing may require several layers to connect nonadjacent resources, and a current-state edge does not by itself identify which earlier upstream pattern predicts a later downstream constraint. In process--logistics systems, transport and buffering can introduce material delays before an upstream disturbance appears as downstream blockage or starvation. Modelling this lag therefore requires both cross-resource interaction and explicit access to relevant history.

\zhpara{现有方法的一项局限在于，模型感受野和可利用的历史证据受构图方式以及空间、时间模块耦合方式制约。局部消息传递可能需要多层才能联系非相邻资源，而当前状态的边本身不能确定哪一种更早的上游模式会预示后续下游约束。在工艺—物流系统中，运输和缓冲会引入物料时延，使上游扰动经过一段时间后才表现为下游阻塞或饥饿。因此，刻画这种滞后既需要跨资源交互，也需要显式利用相关历史。}

\subsection{Transformers and event-oriented forecasting}

The Transformer provides direct interaction among sequence positions through multi-head self-attention and represents order through positional information~\cite{vaswani2017attention}. Compared with recurrent transmission, an earlier observation can influence a later representation through a shorter computational path. This property is useful for long-range time-series dependence, but it does not automatically supply temporal semantics, causality, or delay awareness; input representation, positional design, attention restrictions, and prediction objective remain task-specific~\cite{wen2023transformers}.

\zhpara{Transformer 通过多头自注意力使序列位置直接交互，并利用位置信息表示顺序~\cite{vaswani2017attention}。与循环传递相比，较早观测能够通过更短的计算路径影响后续表示。这一性质有利于建模长距离时间依赖，但不会自动提供时间语义、因果性或延迟感知能力；输入表示、位置设计、注意力约束和预测目标仍需针对任务设计~\cite{wen2023transformers}。}

Graph priors can constrain spatiotemporal attention. GMAN applies separate spatial and temporal attention and uses transform attention to connect historical representations with future steps~\cite{zheng2020gman}. PDFormer further distinguishes geographic and semantic relations and augments geographic attention with recent traffic patterns to model propagation delay~\cite{jiang2023pdformer}. The latter is particularly relevant to manufacturing because a resource may interact with both topological neighbours and behaviourally similar non-neighbours, while a recent upstream pattern may be more informative than the upstream state at the prediction boundary. Nevertheless, an attention weight is a learned association, not direct evidence of a physical transmission time; delay-related claims require controlled comparison and ablation.

\zhpara{图先验可以约束时空注意力。GMAN 分别使用空间与时间注意力，并通过转换注意力连接历史表示与未来时刻~\cite{zheng2020gman}。PDFormer 进一步区分地理关系与语义关系，并以近期交通模式增强地理注意力，从而建模传播时延~\cite{jiang2023pdformer}。后一思路与制造场景尤其相关：资源既可能与拓扑邻居交互，也可能与行为相似的非邻接资源关联，而近期上游模式可能比预测边界时刻的单一上游状态更有信息量。然而，注意力权重只是学习到的关联，并非物理传播时间的直接证据；任何延迟相关结论仍需受控对照与消融实验支持。}

The encoded representation must also match the forecasting target. Most spatiotemporal models produce a future continuous field, whereas operational warnings concern events and their temporal attributes. Predictive process monitoring has jointly estimated the next activity, its timestamp, and remaining case time from event logs~\cite{tax2017predictive}. STGNPP combines graph-based traffic states, event history, a continuous-time GRU, and a point-process intensity to predict the occurrence time and duration of congestion events~\cite{jin2023stgnpp}. These studies demonstrate two alternatives---discrete event decoding and continuous-time intensity modelling---and show that occurrence, onset, duration, and remaining time should be represented explicitly rather than inferred from an unrelated scalar forecast.

\zhpara{编码表示还必须与预测目标匹配。多数时空模型输出未来连续场，而运营预警关注事件及其时间属性。预测性流程监控已利用事件日志联合估计下一活动、对应时间戳和流程剩余时间~\cite{tax2017predictive}；STGNPP 则结合图上的交通状态、事件历史、连续时间 GRU 和点过程强度，预测拥堵事件的发生时间与持续时间~\cite{jin2023stgnpp}。这些研究分别展示了离散事件解码和连续时间强度建模，并表明发生、起点、持续时间和剩余时间应被显式表示，而不应从无关的标量预测中间接推断。}

\subsection{Research gap and positioning}

Three gaps emerge from the reviewed literature. First, delayed cross-resource influence remains incompletely represented in manufacturing bottleneck prediction. Existing recurrent and graph models capture temporal dependence and resource relations, but they do not generally condition cross-station interaction on recurrent historical patterns that precede a propagated constraint. Second, resource representation remains comparatively narrow. Most bottleneck predictors centre on machines, workstations, or management tasks; a unified representation of machines, workers, buffers, gantries, transport robots, and their typed process--logistics relations is less developed. Third, Transformer-based manufacturing bottleneck prediction remains underexplored. Traffic models establish useful mechanisms for long-range and delay-aware interaction, but direct adaptation requires manufacturing-specific state semantics, event definitions, graph relations, and evidence that the mechanisms improve genuine upcoming-event prediction.

\zhpara{上述文献揭示出三项研究空白。第一，制造瓶颈预测对跨资源延迟影响的表示仍不充分。现有循环模型和图模型能够刻画时间依赖与资源关系，但通常不会依据约束传播前反复出现的历史模式来调节跨工位交互。第二，资源表示仍较为狭窄。多数瓶颈预测器以机器、工位或管理任务为中心，对于机器、人员、缓冲区、龙门、运输机器人及其类型化工艺—物流关系的统一表示尚不充分。第三，面向制造瓶颈预测的 Transformer 研究仍较少。交通模型提供了长距离与延迟感知交互机制，但将其迁移到制造场景需要重新定义状态语义、事件、图关系，并通过实验证明这些机制确实改善即将发生事件的预测。}

BNPDFormer is positioned at the intersection of these gaps. It treats bottlenecks as sustained station events rather than isolated score peaks, represents heterogeneous production and logistics resources in one graph, and adapts delay-pattern-enhanced spatial and temporal attention to predict event location, onset, duration, process cause, and order-level consequence. The model does not assume that clustering labels are bottleneck truth or that learned attention is a physical causal path. Instead, these mechanisms are evaluated as representation choices through the protocol and structure analyses reported in Section~\ref{sec:exp}.

\zhpara{BNPDFormer 位于上述研究空白的交叉点：它将瓶颈定义为持续性工位事件而非孤立评分峰值，在同一图中表示异构生产与物流资源，并将延迟模式增强的空间和时间注意力用于预测事件位置、起点、持续时间、过程原因及订单层后果。本文不假设聚类标签就是瓶颈真值，也不把学习到的注意力视为物理因果路径，而是通过第~\ref{sec:exp}节的协议与结构分析，将这些机制作表示选择加以检验。}

\subsection{Production task and resource entities}

This section defines the prediction problem before introducing the learning method. We first specify the production task and heterogeneous entities, then formalize observations and graph relations, construct operational bottleneck events, and finally define the model input, outputs, and report-level success criterion. This ordering separates what must be predicted from how BNPDFormer performs the prediction.

\zhpara{本节在介绍学习方法之前定义预测问题。首先说明生产任务和异构实体，随后形式化观测状态与图关系，再构造运营瓶颈事件，最后定义模型输入、输出及报告级成功条件。这样的顺序将“需要预测什么”与“BNPDFormer 如何完成预测”明确分开。}

We consider a shared digital-manufacturing line that produces water pipes through cutting, grooving, spot welding, root welding, surface welding, and finishing operations. The process is coupled with storage, gantry handling, transport-robot delivery, and human service. The graph implementation represents the primary process backbone with seven workstation nodes because parallel workbench and rotary-welding resources are retained as separate stations. Each production episode contains one order of ten products, and the nominal controller admits at most five products as concurrent WIP. It coordinates admission, process precedence, resource availability, and handling tasks; bottleneck prediction observes this controlled process but does not replace the controller.

\zhpara{本文考虑一条共享式数字制造产线，水管产品依次经过切割、开槽、点焊、根部焊接、表面焊接和后处理等工序，并与仓储、龙门搬运、运输机器人配送及人工服务相耦合。图实现将平行工作台和旋转焊接资源分别保留为独立工位，因此主要工艺骨干由七个工作站节点表示。每个生产 episode 包含一个十件产品订单，名义控制器最多允许五件产品同时作为在制品进入系统，并负责任务准入、工艺先后、资源可用性和搬运任务协调。瓶颈预测对这一受控生产过程进行观测，但不取代底层控制器。}

Let the complete resource set be
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{R}=\mathcal{R}^{m}\cup\mathcal{R}^{g}\cup
\mathcal{R}^{a}\cup\mathcal{R}^{h}\cup\mathcal{R}^{b},
\end{equation}
where the superscripts denote machines and workbenches, gantries, transport robots, humans, and buffers. The retained graph contains 38 nodes: 7 machine/workbench nodes, 4 gantries, 4 transport robots, 5 humans, and 18 buffers. Material is not represented as a separate graph node; its state enters through shortage propagation, inbound waiting, affiliated-buffer occupancy, and other material-flow channels attached to the affected resources. These entities have different operational meanings. A machine transforms a product, a gantry or transport robot moves a product between locations, a human provides labour-dependent service, and a buffer stores intermediate products. The event-supervised target set is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{R}^{m}\cup\mathcal{R}^{g}\cup\mathcal{R}^{a}.
\end{equation}
Human and buffer nodes remain in the observation graph because labour availability and storage occupancy influence other resources, but the current implementation does not assign them station-event targets.

\zhpara{设完整资源集合为 $\mathcal{R}$，其子集分别表示机器与工作台、龙门、运输机器人、人员和缓冲区。最终图共包含三十八个节点，其中包括七个机器/工作台、四个龙门、四个运输机器人、五个人员和十八个缓冲区。物料并不作为独立图节点，而是通过缺料传播、入站等待、关联缓冲区占用及其他物料流通道附着于受影响资源。这些实体具有不同运营含义：机器完成产品加工，龙门或运输机器人负责位置间搬运，人员提供依赖劳动力的服务，缓冲区存放中间产品。事件监督目标集合 $\mathcal{S}$ 由机器、龙门和运输机器人组成。人员和缓冲区仍保留在观测图中，因为劳动力可用性和存储占用会影响其他资源，但当前实现不直接为这两类节点分配工位事件目标。}

\subsection{Heterogeneous process--logistics graph}

The production system is represented as a weighted undirected graph
\begin{equation}
G=(\mathcal{R},E,A),\qquad
E=E^{p}\cup E^{f}\cup E^{b}\cup E^{l}\cup E^{w},
\end{equation}
where $E^{p}$ follows the seven-station process chain, $E^{f}$ connects parallel workstations belonging to the same equipment family, $E^{b}$ links storage buffers to affiliated machines, $E^{l}$ links handling agents to machine resources, and $E^{w}$ provides weak links among gantries or among transport robots. The implemented edge weights are $1.0$, $0.8$, $0.6$, $0.5$, and $0.3$ for these five relation groups, respectively, and every node receives a self-loop of weight $1.0$.

\zhpara{生产系统被表示为加权无向图 $G=(\mathcal{R},E,A)$。其中，$E^{p}$ 沿七个工作站构成工艺链，$E^{f}$ 连接同一设备族内的平行工位，$E^{b}$ 连接存储缓冲区与关联机器，$E^{l}$ 连接搬运主体与机器资源，$E^{w}$ 则在龙门之间或运输机器人之间建立弱连接。代码对上述五组关系分别赋予 $1.0$、$0.8$、$0.6$、$0.5$ 和 $0.3$ 的权重，并为每个节点添加权重为 $1.0$ 的自环。}

The graph is not intended to make the resources homogeneous. Instead, it specifies possible channels through which material flow, service delay, and congestion can propagate. Process edges encode downstream dependency; buffer edges expose storage-mediated blockage or starvation; and logistics edges connect a delayed machine input to the handling resources that can cause it. This distinction is important because two nodes may be structurally close but have different feature semantics, while two structurally distant nodes may exhibit similar operating trajectories. Section~\ref{sec:method} therefore derives both geographic and semantic attention masks from $G$ and the training trajectories.

\zhpara{该图并不假设所有资源是同质的，而是规定物料流、服务延迟和拥堵可能传播的通道。工艺边编码下游依赖，缓冲区边揭示由存储引起的阻塞或饥饿，物流边则把机器入站延迟与可能造成该延迟的搬运资源联系起来。这一区分十分重要：结构相近的节点可能具有不同特征语义，而结构较远的节点也可能呈现相似运行轨迹。因此，第~\ref{sec:method}节将分别从图结构和训练轨迹构建地理注意力与语义注意力掩码。}

\subsection{Windowed production state}

Raw simulation events are sampled at logical-second resolution and aggregated into non-overlapping windows of $\Delta=60$\,s. For resource $n$ and window $t$, the state $x_{t,n}\in\mathbb{R}^{27}$ contains twenty-one operational variables, five resource-type indicators, and one labour-saturation context variable. The operational variables cover queue and occupancy, activity and waiting, blockage and starvation, downtime, inter-departure variability, upstream/downstream interaction, route and inbound delay, shortage propagation, affiliated-buffer occupancy, disturbance context, and two turning-point/momentary indicators. Labour saturation is computed from the human states and broadcast only to machine nodes. The bottleneck score used by the offline aggregation pipeline is deliberately excluded from $x_{t,n}$ to avoid leaking a supervision signal into the predictor.

\zhpara{原始仿真事件以逻辑秒为分辨率采集，并聚合为互不重叠的 $\Delta=60$ 秒窗口。资源 $n$ 在窗口 $t$ 的状态 $x_{t,n}\in\mathbb{R}^{27}$ 由二十一个运营变量、五个资源类型指示变量和一个人力饱和上下文变量组成。运营变量覆盖队列与占用、活跃与等待、阻塞与饥饿、停机、离开间隔波动、上下游交互、路径与入站延迟、缺料传播、关联缓冲区占用、扰动上下文以及转折点和瞬时瓶颈指示。人力饱和状态由人员节点计算并只广播至机器节点。离线聚合流程中的瓶颈评分被明确排除在 $x_{t,n}$ 之外，以避免监督信号泄漏到预测器输入。}

Given $T_{\mathrm{in}}=30$ historical windows over $N$ resources, the model input is
\begin{equation}
X_t=\left[x_{\tau,n}\right]_
{\substack{\tau=t-T_{\mathrm{in}},\ldots,t-1\\n=1,\ldots,N}}
\in\mathbb{R}^{T_{\mathrm{in}}\times N\times 27}.
\end{equation}
The future label grid contains $H_{\mathrm{label}}=20$ one-minute windows. Within this grid, the admissible event onset $H_{\mathrm{start}}$ is varied between experimental settings. Separating the label grid from the onset cap allows an event to begin within the warning range while retaining enough future windows to represent its persistence.

\zhpara{给定覆盖 $N$ 个资源的 $T_{\mathrm{in}}=30$ 个历史窗口，模型输入为张量 $X_t\in\mathbb{R}^{T_{\mathrm{in}}\times N\times27}$。未来标签网格包含 $H_{\mathrm{label}}=20$ 个一分钟窗口，而允许的事件起点上限 $H_{\mathrm{start}}$ 随实验设置变化。将标签范围与起点上限分开，使事件可以在提前预警范围内开始，同时保留足够未来窗口描述其持续过程。}

\subsection{Process-informed bottleneck-event construction}

A bottleneck label must distinguish a production-relevant constraint from ordinary activity or travel. For each supervised resource, the implementation first applies a type-dependent rule
\begin{equation}
b^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t,n}=\Phi_{\rho(n)}(x_{t,n},X_t)\in\{0,1\},
\end{equation}
where $\rho(n)$ is the resource type. For a machine, $\Phi_m$ combines queue accumulation with blocked or starved time, coupled upstream/downstream evidence, shortage propagation, inbound or route delay, downtime, and labour-related waiting. A disturbance flag alone is insufficient: it must coincide with queue, stall, or unavailability evidence. For a gantry, a hot state requires route or inbound delay with stalling, or a stop/unavailable state. For a transport robot, inbound waiting must coincide with stalling, or the robot must be frozen/stopped; normal route travel is not considered a bottleneck.

\zhpara{瓶颈标签必须区分真正影响生产的约束与普通作业或运输活动。对于每个监督资源，代码首先应用资源类型相关规则 $b^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t,n}=\Phi_{\rho(n)}(x_{t,n},X_t)$。对于机器，$\Phi_m$ 将队列积压与阻塞或饥饿时间、上下游耦合证据、缺料传播、入站或路径延迟、停机和劳动力等待结合。单独出现扰动标识不足以形成瓶颈标签，它必须同时伴随队列、停滞或不可用证据。对于龙门，热状态要求路径或入站延迟与停滞同时出现，或者资源处于停止/不可用状态。对于运输机器人，入站等待必须伴随停滞，或机器人处于冻结/停止状态；正常路径运输不被视为瓶颈。}

The raw binary grid is converted into sustained occupancy. In the retained configuration, runs shorter than $m=8$ windows are removed first; an internal cold gap of at most $g=1$ window is then filled when it lies between retained hot regions. Denoting short-run removal by $\mathcal{D}_{m}$ and gap filling by $\mathcal{F}_{g}$, the implemented legacy order is
\begin{equation}
o_{:,n}=\mathcal{F}_{g}\!\left(\mathcal{D}_{m}
\left(b^{\mathrm{raw}}_{:,n}\right)\right).
\end{equation}
This persistence requirement rejects isolated threshold crossings. It should not be confused with the offline event CSV, whose score-event merge can retain a one-window event; BNPDFormer training targets are generated from the operational occupancy grid and use the eight-window criterion.

\zhpara{原始二值网格随后被转换为持续性占用。在最终配置中，首先删除短于 $m=8$ 个窗口的热段；若保留热段之间存在不超过 $g=1$ 个窗口的冷间隙，再将该间隙填补。以 $\mathcal{D}_m$ 表示短段删除、$\mathcal{F}_g$ 表示间隙填补，则代码采用的 legacy 顺序为 $o_{:,n}=\mathcal{F}_{g}(\mathcal{D}_{m}(b^{\mathrm{raw}}_{:,n}))$。该持续性要求用于排除孤立阈值穿越。它不能与离线事件 CSV 的评分事件合并规则混淆：后者可保留一个窗口的事件，而 BNPDFormer 训练目标来自运营占用网格，并采用八窗口条件。}

For a history ending immediately before window $t$, the primary target for station $n$ is
\begin{equation}
y_{t,n}^{\mathrm{evt}}=(w_{t,n},s_{t,n},d_{t,n}),
\end{equation}
where $w_{t,n}$ indicates that an eligible run exists, $s_{t,n}$ is its onset offset, and $d_{t,n}$ is its length within the future grid. If the resource is hot in the final history window and the future run begins at offset zero, the event is \emph{ongoing}; its future remainder may be shorter than eight windows because the full event already satisfied persistence before the observation boundary. Otherwise, a run with $s_{t,n}>0$ is \emph{upcoming} and must satisfy the configured minimum duration. If several runs are available for one station, the longest eligible run is selected rather than the earliest run.

\zhpara{对于紧邻窗口 $t$ 之前结束的历史序列，工位 $n$ 的主要目标为事件三元组 $y_{t,n}^{\mathrm{evt}}=(w_{t,n},s_{t,n},d_{t,n})$。其中，$w_{t,n}$ 表示存在满足条件的事件段，$s_{t,n}$ 为开始偏移，$d_{t,n}$ 为其在未来网格中的长度。如果资源在历史最后一个窗口处于热状态，且未来事件从偏移零开始，则该事件定义为进行中事件；由于完整事件已在观测边界之前满足持续性条件，其未来剩余部分可以短于八个窗口。否则，$s_{t,n}>0$ 的事件定义为即将发生事件，并必须满足最短持续时间。当同一工位存在多个候选热段时，选择最长的合格事件，而不是最早事件。}

\subsection{Cause and order-level targets}

Station events identify where and when production is constrained, but intervention also requires a process explanation. The aggregation pipeline assigns a window-level cause through a fixed evidence priority: material shortage, transport delay, queue buildup, downstream blockage, upstream starvation, and high utilization. The label is inferred from measured shortage, inbound/route delay, queue, blocked, starved, and activity variables; it is not copied from the injected disturbance dimension. This permits, for example, a machine disturbance to create a downstream starvation label after propagation.

\zhpara{工位事件说明生产约束发生在何处以及何时发生，但生产干预还需要过程解释。聚合流程按照固定证据优先级赋予窗口级原因：物料短缺、运输延迟、队列积压、下游阻塞、上游饥饿和高利用率。该标签依据缺料、入站/路径延迟、队列、阻塞、饥饿和活跃变量推导，而不是复制注入扰动维度。因此，例如机器扰动在传播之后可以形成下游饥饿标签。}

The reported cause set is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{C}=\{&
\text{transport delay},\text{material shortage},\\
&\text{upstream starvation},\text{queue buildup}\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Other configured cause indices are ignored by the loss because they lack sufficient train/test support. The order-level target $\ell_t$ is the number of future one-minute windows until all jobs are complete under the configured prediction cap. It provides a line-level estimate of production consequence that complements the station-level event report.

\zhpara{正式报告的原因集合 $\mathcal{C}$ 包括运输延迟、物料短缺、上游饥饿和队列积压。其他已配置原因索引因训练集或测试集支持不足而在损失中被忽略。订单层目标 $\ell_t$ 表示在配置预测上限内，距离全部作业完成还需经过的未来一分钟窗口数。它提供产线层面的生产后果估计，用于补充工位级事件报告。}

\subsection{Prediction mapping and evaluation objective}

The complete prediction problem is the mapping
\begin{equation}
f_{\theta}:(X_t,G)\longmapsto
\left(
\{\hat w_{t,n},\hat s_{t,n},\hat d_{t,n}\}_{n\in\mathcal{S}},
\hat c_t,\hat\ell_t
\right).
\end{equation}
This formulation differs from forecasting a bottleneck score. The output specifies the affected resource, event onset, persistence, process explanation, and expected order impact in a common decision-oriented interface. The method in Section~\ref{sec:method} parameterizes $f_{\theta}$ with a delay-aware heterogeneous spatiotemporal encoder and multiple task heads.

\zhpara{完整预测问题被定义为从历史状态与工厂图到所有监督工位事件、过程原因和订单剩余时间的映射。该形式不同于瓶颈评分预测：输出在统一的决策接口中明确给出受影响资源、事件起点、持续性、过程解释和预期订单影响。第~\ref{sec:method}节使用延迟感知异构时空编码器及多任务预测头参数化该映射。}

The delayed-propagation challenge is contained in this mapping rather than introduced as an additional label. For an upcoming event at target resource $j$, predictive evidence may occur at another resource $i$ and at an earlier history offset $\tau$. The model must therefore learn associations of the form
\begin{equation}
(x_{t-\tau,i},\,i\!\rightarrow\!j)\longmapsto y^{\mathrm{evt}}_{t,j},
\qquad \tau>0,
\end{equation}
where $i\!\rightarrow\!j$ denotes a possible process or logistics route, not a proven physical causal path. This formulation motivates conditioning cross-resource interaction on recurrent historical patterns while evaluating success only through the future event report.

\zhpara{延迟传播困难包含在上述映射中，而不是通过额外标签引入。对于目标资源 $j$ 的 upcoming 事件，可预测证据可能出现在另一资源 $i$ 及更早的历史偏移 $\tau$ 处。因此，模型需要学习从较早资源状态与可能的工艺或物流路径到未来目标事件的关联。这里的 $i\!\rightarrow\!j$ 表示潜在传播通道，而不是已经证明的物理因果路径。该形式化为“使用重复历史模式调节跨资源交互”提供动机，而模型成败仍只通过未来事件报告进行评价。}

Two event criteria are distinguished. A station-level hit requires that the predicted resource equal the labelled resource. A stricter report-level true positive additionally requires
\begin{equation}
|\hat{s}_{t,n}-s_{t,n}|\leq\epsilon_s,\qquad
\epsilon_s=3~\mathrm{min}.
\end{equation}
Occurrence precision, recall, and F1 quantify the coverage and reliability of alarms. Start, duration, and remaining-time errors are conditional or regression measures and cannot compensate for a missed station event. Ongoing and upcoming recalls are therefore reported separately: the latter is the direct measure of whether the model provides warning before the bottleneck becomes visible at the target station.

\zhpara{本文区分两种事件判定标准。工位级命中要求预测资源与标签资源一致；更严格的报告级真阳性还要求开始时间误差不超过 $\epsilon_s=3$ 分钟。事件精确率、召回率和 F1 衡量报警的可靠性与覆盖程度；开始时间、持续时间和订单剩余时间误差属于条件性或回归指标，不能弥补工位事件漏检。因此，本文分别报告进行中与即将发生事件的召回率，其中后者直接衡量模型能否在瓶颈于目标工位显现之前提供预警。}

\subsection{Method overview}

BNPDFormer is organized around the three contributions established in Section~\ref{sec:intro}. First, controlled simulation, a heterogeneous factory graph, and grouped embedding integrate machine, human, material-flow, buffer, gantry, and transport evidence. Second, process-informed event construction and recurrent-pattern grouping address scarce, weakly separable supervision while retaining traceability to observable production evidence. Third, pattern-enhanced spatiotemporal attention addresses delayed propagation. The complete information flow is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{simulation}&\rightarrow\text{window/event construction}\\
&\rightarrow\text{factory graph}\rightarrow\text{joint prediction}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The first two stages create reproducible observations and targets. The latter two parameterize the mapping $f_\theta$ defined in Section~\ref{sec:problem}.

\zhpara{BNPDFormer 围绕第~\ref{sec:intro}节提出的三项贡献组织。第一，可控仿真、异构工厂图和分组嵌入联合机器、人员、物料流、缓冲区、龙门和运输证据；第二，过程启发式事件构造与重复模式分组面向正样本稀缺和监督可分性不足，并保持预测对可观测生产证据的可追溯性；第三，模式增强时空注意力用于处理延迟传播。完整信息流为“仿真生成—窗口与事件构造—工厂图—联合预测”。前两个阶段形成可复现观测与目标，后两个阶段参数化第~\ref{sec:problem}节定义的映射 $f_\theta$。}

Figure~\ref{fig:method-flow} organizes the method into four modules. The environment is a disturbance-controlled water-pipe line. Data processing converts its logs into minute windows, process-informed sustained labels, and clustered short-term patterns. BNPDFormer then encodes these inputs with grouped embeddings and temporal, graph, and semantic attention, using the clustered patterns to condition delayed cross-station interaction. The report states the bottleneck station, onset, duration, process cause, and remaining order time.

\zhpara{图~\ref{fig:method-flow} 将方法组织为四个模块。环境是一条可控扰动的水管产线。数据处理把日志转为分钟窗口、过程启发式持续性标签和短时模式聚类。BNPDFormer 再用分组嵌入以及时间、图和语义注意力编码这些输入，并用聚类模式调节跨工位的延迟交互。报告给出瓶颈工位、开始时间、持续时间、过程原因和订单剩余时间。}

\subsection{Disturbance-controlled digital production line}

The data-generation environment reconstructs the water-pipe line, resource layout, storage locations, and handling activities in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. At every logical second, the collector records the episode configuration, active disturbance, resource-state transitions, job traces, buffer events, transport tasks, and material inventory. These streams are synchronized by episode and logical time, allowing a downstream event to be traced back through the states that preceded it. Only episodes in which all ten products complete are exported, and episodes containing a deadlock reset are removed.

\zhpara{数据生成环境在 NVIDIA Isaac Sim 中重建水管产线、资源布局、存储位置和搬运活动。采集器在每个逻辑秒记录 episode 配置、当前扰动、资源状态迁移、作业轨迹、缓冲区事件、运输任务和物料库存。这些数据流按 episode 与逻辑时间同步，使下游事件能够沿先前状态进行追溯。只有十件产品全部完成的 episode 才会被导出，包含 deadlock reset 的 episode 则被剔除。}

Formal single-axis collection activates one disturbance dimension at intensity $I=1.0$ while leaving the other dimensions nominal:
\begin{itemize}
 \item[$\bullet$] \textbf{Machine disturbance} increases processing-time variation, lowers successful operation probability, disables selected workstations, and inserts temporary machine-fault pulses.
 \item[$\bullet$] \textbf{Human disturbance} reduces the workforce from five to three, increases task-time variation, and inserts temporary leave periods.
 \item[$\bullet$] \textbf{Logistics disturbance} reduces transport robots from four to two, slows gantry motion, and inserts handling freezes that include an AGV freeze.
 \item[$\bullet$] \textbf{Material disturbance} temporarily hides flange or elbow kits and introduces additional shortage pulses for selected stock-keeping units.
\end{itemize}
The temporary machine, human, and logistics pulses last at least ten logical minutes. This design does not assume that the injected dimension is the final bottleneck cause. Its purpose is to produce traceable perturbations whose operational consequences can emerge at different resources and times.

\zhpara{正式单轴采集以强度 $I=1.0$ 每次只激活一个扰动维度，其余维度保持名义状态。机器扰动增加加工时间波动、降低成功执行概率、关闭部分工位并注入临时故障脉冲；人员扰动将工人数由五人减至三人、增加任务时间波动并注入临时离岗；物流扰动将运输机器人由四台减至两台、降低龙门速度并注入包含 AGV 冻结的搬运中断；物料扰动暂时隐藏法兰或弯头套件，并对选定库存单元增加短缺脉冲。机器、人员和物流临时脉冲至少持续十个逻辑分钟。该设计并不把注入维度视为最终瓶颈原因，其目的在于产生可追溯扰动，使其运营后果能够在不同资源和时间上显现。}

Mixed two-, three-, and four-dimension episodes are exported as a separate dataset. They are not merged into the retained single-axis dataset because simultaneous shocks change both event prevalence and the attribution problem. This separation permits the main model to be trained on interpretable perturbation trajectories and allows mixed disturbances to be examined later as a distribution-expansion experiment.

\zhpara{二维、三维和四维混合扰动 episode 被导出为独立数据集，而不直接并入最终单轴数据集，因为同时冲击会同时改变事件发生率和原因归属问题。这样的分离使主模型能够在更易追踪的扰动轨迹上训练，并将混合扰动作为后续分布扩展实验单独考察。}

\subsection{Multi-entity aggregation and process-consistent supervision}

The second stage converts heterogeneous raw logs into a common resource--time grid. Statistics are aggregated over non-overlapping one-minute windows and aligned by resource identifier. Missing resource states are completed through the export pipeline, type one-hot variables are appended, and labour saturation is recomputed from the available human nodes. The resulting tensor contains 38 resource nodes and 27 channels in the retained dataset. Episode boundaries are preserved throughout sliding-window generation, preventing a history from combining observations from two production runs.

\zhpara{第二阶段将异构原始日志转换为统一的资源—时间网格。统计量按互不重叠的一分钟窗口聚合并依据资源标识对齐；缺失资源状态由导出流程补齐，随后加入类型独热变量，并依据可用人员节点重新计算人力饱和状态。最终保留数据集包含三十八个资源节点和二十七个通道。滑动窗口生成过程中始终保留 episode 边界，避免一个历史样本拼接两个不同生产运行。}

Event supervision follows the type-specific occupancy and persistence definitions in Section~\ref{sec:problem}. The retained configuration uses operational-state targets rather than the exported bottleneck-score column. Consequently, the model cannot solve the task by reproducing the score used during aggregation. The event target builder scans each station's future occupancy, rejects ineligible upcoming runs, preserves the short remainder of an already established ongoing event, applies the configured onset cap, and selects the longest eligible run. This produces mutually aligned occurrence, onset, duration, occupancy-grid, and prefix targets.

\zhpara{事件监督遵循第~\ref{sec:problem}节定义的资源类型占用与持续性规则。最终配置使用运营状态生成目标，而不使用导出的瓶颈评分列，因此模型不能通过复现聚合阶段的评分来完成任务。事件目标构造器扫描每个工位的未来占用，删除不合格 upcoming 热段，保留已经建立的 ongoing 事件的短剩余部分，应用配置起点上限，并选择最长合格热段，由此形成彼此对齐的发生、起点、持续时间、未来占用网格和 prefix 目标。}

Three different clustering-related objects are kept conceptually separate. First, the offline aggregation tools can produce station- and window-level $K=8$ k-means descriptors for exploratory regime analysis. Second, the training dataset derives cause-aligned regime identifiers by deterministic rules and can use them for representation conditioning and contrastive regularization. Third, short temporal patches are clustered to create the pattern keys used by geographic attention. Only the third object is an active clustering mechanism in the retained delay module. The offline cluster-prediction loss has weight zero, and no cluster index defines the bottleneck ground truth; event labels are produced only by the process rules and persistence filter above.

\zhpara{本文明确区分三类与“聚类”相关的对象。第一，离线聚合工具可以产生工位级和窗口级的 $K=8$ k-means 描述，用于探索性工况分析；第二，训练数据依据确定性规则生成原因对齐工况标识，可用于表示条件化与对比正则；第三，对短时间 patch 进行聚类，以形成地理注意力使用的模式键。最终延迟模块中只有第三类属于正在使用的聚类机制。离线簇预测损失权重为零，且任何簇编号都不构成瓶颈真值；事件标签只由上述过程规则和持续性过滤得到。}

\subsection{Factory graph, geographic mask, and semantic mask}

The weighted adjacency $A$ follows the relation definitions in Section~\ref{sec:problem}. Floyd--Warshall shortest-path computation converts nonzero edges into hop distances $D^{\mathrm{hop}}$. The geographic mask is
\begin{equation}
M^G_{ij}=
\begin{cases}
0, & D^{\mathrm{hop}}_{ij}<\delta_G,\\
-\infty, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\qquad \delta_G=4,
\end{equation}
so a node attends only to resources fewer than four hops away in geographic attention. This mask allows information to cross more than one process or logistics edge while excluding globally unrelated nodes.

\zhpara{加权邻接矩阵 $A$ 遵循第~\ref{sec:problem}节的关系定义。Floyd--Warshall 最短路径计算将非零边转换为跳数距离 $D^{\mathrm{hop}}$。地理掩码仅允许最短路径小于 $\delta_G=4$ 的节点相互注意，使信息可以跨越不止一条工艺或物流边传播，同时排除全局无关节点。}

Geographic proximity alone cannot capture parallel or substitutable resources with similar behaviour. A semantic distance matrix is therefore computed from training trajectories. The feature channels of each node are first averaged to form a scalar trajectory $\bar x_{:,i}$, after which
\begin{equation}
D^{\mathrm{sem}}_{ij}
=1-\mathrm{corr}(\bar{x}_{:,i},\bar{x}_{:,j}).
\end{equation}
For each node, the five nearest trajectories are unmasked in $M^S$; all others are suppressed. This implementation uses Pearson correlation distance as a short-episode substitute for the dynamic-time-warping distance used in traffic PDFormer. Finally, the nontrivial eigenvectors of the normalized graph Laplacian provide an eight-dimensional structural position for every resource.

\zhpara{仅依赖地理邻近无法表示行为相似的平行或可替代资源，因此模型还依据训练轨迹构建语义距离矩阵。首先对每个节点的特征通道取平均形成标量轨迹 $\bar x_{:,i}$，再计算 $D^{\mathrm{sem}}_{ij}=1-\mathrm{corr}(\bar{x}_{:,i},\bar{x}_{:,j})$。对于每个节点，语义掩码 $M^S$ 仅保留距离最近的五条轨迹。该实现以 Pearson 相关距离替代交通 PDFormer 中适合长交通序列的动态时间规整距离。最后，归一化图拉普拉斯的非平凡特征向量为每个资源提供八维结构位置。}

\subsection{Grouped heterogeneous state embedding}

Directly projecting all 27 channels with one linear layer would treat queue length, downtime, type codes, and shortage evidence as if they shared one measurement semantics. The grouped token embedding instead partitions the channels into queue, stall, logistics, shortage, context, and type groups. Each group $q$ has an independent affine projection and GELU activation:
\begin{equation}
e^{(q)}_{t,n}=\mathrm{GELU}
\left(W_q x^{(q)}_{t,n}+b_q\right).
\end{equation}
The six group embeddings are mean-pooled, rather than concatenated as separate tokens:
\begin{equation}
e_{t,n}=\frac{1}{6}\sum_{q=1}^{6}e^{(q)}_{t,n}.
\end{equation}
This operation places heterogeneous evidence in a common latent space while preventing a high-dimensional group from dominating solely through channel count.

\zhpara{若直接使用单个线性层投影全部二十七个通道，就会把队列长度、停机时间、类型编码和缺料证据视为具有相同测量语义。分组 token 嵌入将通道划分为队列、停滞、物流、缺料、上下文和类型六组，每组分别经过仿射投影和 GELU 激活。六组嵌入随后取平均，而不是拼接成独立 token。该操作在保留不同物理语义的同时将异构证据映射到共同潜空间，并避免某一高维组仅因通道数量较多而占据主导。}

The initial representation combines grouped evidence with temporal, structural, and identity information:
\begin{equation}
H^{(0)}=E_{\mathrm{group}}(X_t)+
E_{\mathrm{time}}+E_{\mathrm{lap}}(G)+E_{\mathrm{node}}.
\end{equation}
$E_{\mathrm{time}}$ is sinusoidal, $E_{\mathrm{lap}}$ projects the graph eigenvectors, and $E_{\mathrm{node}}$ is a learned resource-identity embedding. Day-of-week and time-of-day embeddings are disabled because an episode is driven by order progress and disturbances rather than calendar periodicity.

\zhpara{初始表示由分组证据、时间信息、图结构和节点身份共同组成。$E_{\mathrm{time}}$ 为正弦时间位置编码，$E_{\mathrm{lap}}$ 投影图拉普拉斯特征向量，$E_{\mathrm{node}}$ 为可学习资源身份嵌入。模型关闭星期和日内时刻编码，因为 episode 演化由订单进度和扰动驱动，而不是由日历周期驱动。}

\subsection{Delay-pattern-enhanced spatiotemporal encoder}

The encoder must distinguish three dependencies: evolution of one resource through time, propagation through the process--logistics graph, and similarity between resources that need not be adjacent. Each encoder block therefore allocates separate attention heads to temporal, geographic, and semantic attention. For an attention branch $b\in\{T,G,S\}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Attn}_{b}(Q_b,K_b,V_b;M_b)
=\mathrm{softmax}\!\left(
\frac{Q_bK_b^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}+M_b
\right)V_b ,
\end{equation}
where temporal attention has no cross-node mask, geographic attention uses $M^G$, and semantic attention uses $M^S$.

\zhpara{编码器需要区分三类依赖：单个资源随时间的演化、沿工艺—物流图的传播，以及不一定相邻资源之间的行为相似性。因此，每个编码器模块分别为时间、地理和语义注意力分配注意力头。时间注意力不使用跨节点掩码，地理注意力使用 $M^G$，语义注意力使用 $M^S$。}

Delayed propagation is introduced through data-derived short-pattern keys. In the retained implementation, the prototype bank is fitted by applying k-means with sixteen centres to length-three patches of the configured activity channel (\texttt{active\_pct\_s}), whereas online query patches use the first model-output channel (\texttt{queue\_length\_s}). Let $P_{t,n}$ denote a queue query patch and $K^P$ the learned activity-pattern centres. Pattern attention computes
\begin{equation}
C^P_{t,n}=
\mathrm{softmax}\!\left(
\frac{Q^P(P_{t,n})K^P(K^P)^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}
\right)V^P(K^P).
\end{equation}
The resulting cross-channel pattern context is added to the geographic key before geographic attention is evaluated. Thus, spatial interaction is conditioned not only on node proximity but also on the learned association between current queue evolution and recurrent activity prototypes. The mechanism does not use a Fourier transform.

\zhpara{延迟传播通过数据驱动的短模式键引入。在最终实现中，模式原型库依据指定活动通道（\texttt{active\_pct\_s}）的长度三 patch，通过十六中心 k-means 拟合；在线查询 patch 则来自模型的第一个输出通道（\texttt{queue\_length\_s}）。模式注意力将当前队列查询与活动模式原型进行投影匹配，所得跨通道模式上下文在计算地理注意力之前加入地理 key。因此，空间交互不仅取决于节点邻近性，还取决于当前队列演化与重复活动原型之间学习到的关联。该机制不使用傅里叶变换。}

For encoder block $l$, the three branches are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
H_T^{(l)}&=\mathrm{Attn}_T(H^{(l-1)}),\\
H_G^{(l)}&=\mathrm{Attn}_G(H^{(l-1)},M^G,C^P),\\
H_S^{(l)}&=\mathrm{Attn}_S(H^{(l-1)},M^S).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Their outputs are concatenated, projected, and processed by pre-normalized residual and feed-forward layers. The retained configuration uses five blocks with latent dimension 80. It allocates four temporal heads, two geographic heads, and two semantic heads; these are separate branches rather than one undifferentiated eight-head attention module.

\zhpara{在第 $l$ 个编码器模块中，时间、地理和语义分支分别生成 $H_T^{(l)}$、$H_G^{(l)}$ 和 $H_S^{(l)}$。三者输出经过拼接和投影，再进入预归一化残差与前馈层。最终配置使用五个编码器模块和八十维潜表示，并分别分配四个时间头、两个地理头和两个语义头；它们是三类独立分支，而不是一个未区分作用的八头注意力模块。}

\subsection{Event, occupancy, and explanatory heads}

Let $z_{t,n}$ be the final representation of supervised station $n$. The event occurrence module contains separate logits for continuation and future onset:
\begin{equation}
p^{\mathrm{cont}}_{t,n}=\sigma(f_{\mathrm{cont}}(z_{t,n})),
\qquad
p^{\mathrm{onset}}_{t,n}=\sigma(f_{\mathrm{onset}}(z_{t,n})).
\end{equation}
The history-end occupancy selects which logit is used for the combined occurrence probability. This split prevents the abundant ongoing pattern from dominating the weaker precursor pattern of an upcoming event. A start head produces a categorical distribution over future minute offsets, while duration and prefix heads produce nonnegative lengths:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\pi^s_{t,n}&=\mathrm{softmax}(f_s(z_{t,n})),\\
\hat d_{t,n}&=\mathrm{softplus}(f_d(z_{t,n})),\\
\hat p_{t,n}&=\mathrm{softplus}(f_p(z_{t,n})).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\zhpara{设 $z_{t,n}$ 为监督工位 $n$ 的最终表示。事件发生模块分别输出持续事件 logit 与未来起点 logit，并依据历史末端占用状态选择组合发生概率。这种分离避免数量较多的 ongoing 模式压制 upcoming 事件中较弱的前驱模式。开始时间头输出未来分钟偏移上的分类分布，持续时间头和 prefix 头则输出非负长度。}

An occupancy decoder predicts the complete future hot grid $\hat O_t\in[0,1]^{H_{\mathrm{label}}\times N}$. It acts as dense temporal supervision for the sparse event tuple. The prefix prediction estimates how long a hot segment continues from the first future window and is used to distinguish a truly continuing bottleneck from a station that was hot at the end of history but immediately recovers. A precursor branch additionally combines local queue/stall evidence with neighbourhood information before occurrence prediction.

\zhpara{占用解码器预测完整未来热状态网格 $\hat O_t\in[0,1]^{H_{\mathrm{label}}\times N}$，为稀疏事件三元组提供密集时间监督。prefix 预测估计从第一个未来窗口开始热段连续维持多久，用于区分真正持续的瓶颈与历史末端虽为热状态但随即恢复的工位。前驱分支还在事件发生预测前融合局部队列/停滞证据与邻域信息。}

Order remaining time and cause are graph-level outputs. The final node states are pooled through mean aggregation and a maximum gated-node score, then concatenated with order-progress information:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
u_t&=\mathrm{AuxPool}(H_t^{(L)},\text{order state}),\\
\hat\ell_t&=f_\ell(u_t),\qquad \hat c_t=f_c(u_t).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The cause head retains ten output indices for checkpoint compatibility, but training and inference are restricted to the four supported report classes. This preserves implementation compatibility without presenting unsupported classes as evaluated predictions.

\zhpara{订单剩余时间和过程原因属于图级输出。最终节点状态通过均值聚合与最大门控节点评分进行池化，并与订单进度信息拼接得到 $u_t$，再分别输入剩余时间头和原因头。为兼容已有 checkpoint，原因头保留十个输出索引，但训练和推理限制在四个具有样本支持的报告类别内，从而在保持实现兼容性的同时避免把无样本类别表述为已评估预测。}

\subsection{Joint training objective}

The learning objective is organized into primary event terms, dense temporal terms, explanatory terms, and representation regularizers:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}={}&
\lambda_w\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{will}}+
\lambda_{on}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{onset}}+
\lambda_s\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{start}}+
\lambda_d\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{dur}}\\
&+\lambda_p\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{prefix}}+
\lambda_o\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{occ}}+
\lambda_{\ell}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{remain}}+
\lambda_c\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cause}}\\
&+\lambda_r\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{recon}}+
\lambda_q\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{contrast}}+
\lambda_{\mathrm{tpm}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tpm}} .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The occurrence loss combines reweighted binary cross-entropy, focal modulation, hard-false-positive weighting, and an internal differentiable F1 term. Separate onset supervision is applied to the cold-history head. The start loss uses a Gaussian soft target over minute offsets, whereas duration, prefix, and remaining time use log-transformed Smooth-L1 regression. Remaining-time samples are further weighted by order progress.

\zhpara{联合目标由主要事件项、密集时间项、解释性任务项和表示正则项组成。发生损失结合重加权二元交叉熵、focal 调制、困难假阳性加权和内部可微 F1 项；冷历史分支另接受独立起点监督。开始时间损失采用分钟偏移上的高斯软目标，持续时间、prefix 和订单剩余时间采用对数变换后的 Smooth-L1 回归，剩余时间样本还依据订单进度进行加权。}

The occupancy term combines type-balanced binary cross-entropy, soft Dice, and soft intersection-over-union losses. Cause classification uses class-weighted cross-entropy only for supported hot-window labels. Future-feature reconstruction and graph-level supervised contrastive learning regularize the shared representation, while an auxiliary turning-point/momentary signal contributes a binary loss. In the retained configuration, legacy score, will, mark, time-to-start, STGNPP, and offline cluster-head losses are all disabled. In particular, $\lambda_{\mathrm{cluster}}=0$; the headline event prediction is not optimized to reproduce offline k-means assignments.

\zhpara{未来占用项由资源类型平衡的二元交叉熵、soft Dice 和 soft IoU 组成。原因分类只对具有支持且处于热状态的窗口使用类别加权交叉熵。未来特征重构和图级监督对比学习用于正则化共享表示，转折点/瞬时信号则提供辅助二元损失。在最终配置中，旧版 score、will、mark、time-to-start、STGNPP 和离线簇头损失均被关闭。尤其是 $\lambda_{\mathrm{cluster}}=0$，核心事件预测不会被优化为复现离线 k-means 分配。}

\subsection{Validation-controlled decoding}

Training loss alone does not define an operational report. At inference, BNPDFormer first chooses the continuation logit for historically hot stations and the onset logit for historically cold stations. Prefix evidence can lift an ongoing occurrence and set its onset to zero. Predicted occupancy runs can be united with the event head when they satisfy the minimum-duration rule, including a separate upcoming union. Type-specific report thresholds are retained for gantries and transport robots, and events whose predicted onset exceeds the active cap are removed.

\zhpara{训练损失本身并不能完整定义运营报告。推理时，BNPDFormer 对历史热工位使用持续事件 logit，对历史冷工位使用未来起点 logit；prefix 证据可以提升 ongoing 发生概率并将其起点设为零；满足最短持续时间的预测占用段可以与事件头取并集，其中 upcoming 事件采用独立并集逻辑。模型还为龙门和运输机器人保留资源类型相关报告阈值，并删除预测起点超过当前上限的事件。}

The global report threshold is not tuned on the test set. Candidate thresholds are swept on validation data. The retained checkpoint must satisfy the configured validation precision and recall gate when possible, after which station F1 determines selection; if no candidate opens the gate, the documented fallback rule is used. The chosen checkpoint and threshold are then frozen for held-out evaluation. This separation is essential because the decoder contains several event-consistency rules and could otherwise be inadvertently fitted to the test distribution.

\zhpara{全局报告阈值不会在测试集上调节。候选阈值只在验证集上扫描；在条件允许时，入选 checkpoint 必须满足配置的验证精确率与召回率门槛，再依据工位 F1 进行选择；若没有候选值通过门槛，则采用预先记录的 fallback 规则。入选 checkpoint 与阈值随后冻结并用于留出测试。由于解码器包含多项事件一致性规则，这种分离能够避免解码过程被无意拟合到测试分布。}

\section{Experiments}\label{sec:exp}

This chapter evaluates BNPDFormer in the order required to assess the complete method: the data and common protocol are first fixed, the retained model is then evaluated on event, temporal, and auxiliary tasks, and the same metrics are used to compare representative baselines. Ablation studies isolate heterogeneous observations, process-informed data construction, and delay-pattern conditioning. A traceability case study and a generalization analysis complete the empirical evaluation.

\zhpara{本章按照完整评价流程组织实验：首先固定数据与统一实验协议，随后在事件、时间和辅助任务上评价最终模型，并使用相同指标与具有代表性的基线进行比较；接着通过消融实验分别考察异构观测、过程启发式数据构造和延迟模式条件化的作用；最后以过程可追溯案例和泛化分析补充实验结论。}

\subsection{Experimental setup}

\subsubsection{Dataset and data processing}

The experiments use the \texttt{dense\_i1} digital-twin production-line dataset described in Section~\ref{sec:method}. It contains 204 complete episodes and 38 simulated resources, including machines, workbenches, gantries, transport robots, workers, and buffers. Episodes, rather than individual windows, are split into 140/28/36 training, validation, and test episodes, yielding 20,826/4,331/5,354 one-minute windows. Each input contains 30 historical windows (30 min) and a 20-window future grid (20 min). The test set therefore contains 5,354 windows evaluated over 15 supervised target resources: seven machines or workbenches, four gantries, and four transport robots. Worker and buffer states remain in the input as contextual observations but are not treated as direct prediction targets.

\zhpara{实验使用第~\ref{sec:method}节介绍的 \texttt{dense\_i1} 数字孪生产线数据集。该数据集包含二百零四个完整 episode 和三十八个仿真资源，包括机器、工作台、龙门、运输机器人、人员和缓冲区。本文按 episode 而不是按窗口划分数据，训练集、验证集和测试集分别包含 140/28/36 个 episode，对应 20,826/4,331/5,354 个一分钟窗口。每个输入包含三十个历史窗口（三十分钟）和二十个未来标签窗口（二十分钟）。因此，测试集在十五个监督目标资源上进行评估：七个机器或工作台、四个龙门和四个运输机器人。人员和缓冲区状态作为上下文观测保留在输入中，但不作为直接预测目标。}

\subsubsection{Overall experimental settings and metrics}

The retained BNPDFormer follows the latest evaluation configuration of the factory bottleneck pipeline. It uses five encoder blocks, an 80-dimensional latent representation, eight-dimensional Laplacian positional encoding, $4/2/2$ temporal/geographic/semantic attention heads, a four-hop geographic mask, five semantic neighbours, and 16 length-three pattern keys. AdamW is trained with batch size 16, learning rate $5\times10^{-5}$, weight decay $0.05$, cosine decay to $10^{-6}$, and fourfold positive-window oversampling. The retained checkpoint is warm-started and trained for at most 100 epochs; the three onset-cap tasks are selected independently after validation-controlled decoding. All reported runs use seed 42.

\zhpara{最终 BNPDFormer 采用工厂瓶颈预测流程的最新评测配置。模型包含五个编码器模块、八十维潜表示、八维拉普拉斯位置编码、$4/2/2$ 的时间/地理/语义注意力头、四跳地理掩码、五个语义邻居和十六个长度为三的模式键。训练使用 AdamW，batch size 为 16，学习率为 $5\times10^{-5}$，权重衰减为 $0.05$，余弦衰减下限为 $10^{-6}$，并对正例窗口进行四倍过采样。最终 checkpoint 使用热启动，最多训练一百个 epoch；三个起点范围分别在验证集控制的解码流程下选择模型。所有报告运行均使用 seed 42。}

The common metrics are defined as follows. \emph{Will15 F1} measures station identification over the 15 target resources. \emph{Report F1} additionally requires the predicted onset to be within three windows of the labelled onset. Upcoming and ongoing recall separate early warning from recognition of an already active bottleneck. State F1 is reported for the next window and for the complete 20-window horizon. Start MAE and duration MAE are computed on correctly detected events; the union duration MAE/RMSE additionally penalizes missed and false events and is used when available. Remaining-order-time MAE is a global line-level metric, while cause accuracy and macro recall are computed over the four supported process-cause classes. Checkpoints and thresholds are chosen on the validation set and then frozen for the held-out test set.

\zhpara{统一指标定义如下。\emph{Will15 F1} 衡量十五个目标资源上的工位识别；\emph{Report F1} 进一步要求预测起点与标签起点相差不超过三个窗口。Upcoming 召回率衡量提前预警，ongoing 召回率衡量对已发生瓶颈的持续识别。State F1 分别报告下一窗口和完整二十窗口范围的结果。起点 MAE 与持续时间 MAE 在正确识别的事件上计算；union 持续时间 MAE/RMSE 还会惩罚漏报和误报，并在有统一记录时报告。订单剩余时间 MAE 是产线级指标，原因准确率和宏平均召回率在四个有样本支持的过程原因类别上计算。checkpoint 与阈值仅在验证集上选择，随后冻结并用于留出测试集。}

\subsection{Discussion and validity boundaries}

The main comparison supports three empirical conclusions. First, heterogeneous observations and cross-type relations are most valuable for upcoming warning: machine-only input retains active-event recall but loses the precursors needed before a target station becomes constrained. Second, process-informed persistence and recurrent regime grouping improve the separability of bottleneck and normal trajectories, which is reflected in report F1 and one-step state F1. Third, delay-pattern conditioning is associated with stronger long-range event and queue-buildup prediction under the recorded training protocol. The case study shows that these gains correspond to contiguous resource-level reports that can be checked against observed production traces.

\zhpara{主实验和消融实验共同支持三个经验结论。第一，异构观测与跨类型关系对于 upcoming 预警最有价值：仅机器输入能够保持对已发生事件的召回，却会丢失目标工位形成约束之前所需的前驱信息。第二，过程启发式持续性约束与重复工况分组提高了瓶颈轨迹和正常轨迹的可分性，并体现在 Report F1 和下一窗口 State F1 上。第三，在当前训练协议下，延迟模式条件化与更好的远距离事件和队列积压预测相关。案例分析进一步显示，这些提升对应于可以与生产轨迹核对的连续资源级报告。}

Several boundaries qualify the interpretation. All runs use one simulated production line and one random seed. The retained model is warm-started for up to 100 epochs, whereas several ablations are cold-started with shorter budgets, and masking entity channels changes the event pool. The grouping experiment is a data-pipeline comparison with different episode composition, and the no-pattern results use a separate recorded run. The cause head is evaluated only over four supported observable classes, and baseline auxiliary metrics are aligned through the global remaining-time MAE rather than through model-specific primary or progress-weighted variants. Finally, the current archive does not support a verified efficiency ranking or cross-factory generalization claim. The reported results therefore establish contribution-level evidence under a controlled digital-twin protocol, while equal-budget multi-seed experiments, matched efficiency measurements, and transfer to another factory remain necessary for stronger claims.

\zhpara{上述解释受到多项边界限制。所有运行均来自同一条仿真产线和同一个随机种子。最终模型采用最多一百个 epoch 的热启动，而部分消融采用较短预算的冷启动；屏蔽实体通道还会改变事件池。分组实验属于 episode 组成不同的数据流程比较，无模式结果也来自独立记录的运行。原因头只在四个有样本支持的可观测类别上评估；基线辅助指标通过全局剩余时间 MAE 对齐，不使用各模型特有的 primary 或 progress-weighted 变体进行横向排序。最后，当前档案不支持经过核验的效率排名或跨工厂泛化结论。因此，本文结果建立的是受控数字孪生协议下的贡献层证据；若要形成更强结论，还需要等预算多随机种子实验、匹配效率测量以及另一条产线上的迁移验证。}
