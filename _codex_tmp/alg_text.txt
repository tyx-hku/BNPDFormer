4.2. Disturbance-controlled digital production
line
The data-generation environment reconstructs the
water-pipe line, resource layout, storage locations, and
handling activities in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. At every
logical second, the collector records the episode config-
uration, active disturbance, resource-state transitions,
job traces, buffer events, transport tasks, and material

and logical time, allowing a downstream event to
be traced back through the states that preceded it.
Only episodes in which all ten products complete are
exported, and episodes containing a deadlock reset are

Formal single-axis collection activates one distur-
bance dimension at intensity 𝐼= 1.0 while leaving the
other dimensions nominal:
∙Machine disturbance increases processing-time
variation, lowers successful operation probability,
disables selected workstations, and inserts tempo-
rary machine-fault pulses.
∙Human disturbance reduces the workforce from
five to three, increases task-time variation, and
inserts temporary leave periods.
∙Logistics disturbance reduces transport robots
from four to two, slows gantry motion, and inserts
handling freezes that include an AGV freeze.
∙Material disturbance temporarily hides flange or
elbow kits and introduces additional shortage
pulses for selected stock-keeping units.
The temporary machine, human, and logistics pulses
last at least ten logical minutes. This design does
not assume that the injected dimension is the final
bottleneck cause. Its purpose is to produce trace-
able perturbations whose operational consequences can
emerge at different resources and times.

4.9. Validation-controlled decoding
Training loss alone does not define an operational
report. At inference, PHGST-DP first chooses the
continuation logit for historically hot stations and
the onset logit for historically cold stations. Prefix
evidence can lift an ongoing occurrence and set its
onset to zero. Predicted occupancy runs can be united
with the event head when they satisfy the minimum-
duration rule, including a separate upcoming union.
Type-specific report thresholds are retained for gantries
and transport robots, and events whose predicted onset
exceeds the active cap are removed.
训练损失本身并不能完整定义运营报告。推理时，PHGST-DP
对历史热工位使用持续事件logit，对历史冷工位使用未来起
点logit；prefix 证据可以提升ongoing 发生概率并将其起点
